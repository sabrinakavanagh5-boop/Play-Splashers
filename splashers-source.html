Splashers

Something under the water hears everything. Swing across the flooded halls and find the tunnel out. Make a sound in the water and something very fast will hunt you down.

Start at level:
Move like a gorilla: push your hands against platforms and pillars to shove yourself along. Swing your arms to run, push down hard to jump. Your hands stick to anything they touch. The platforms are separate islands, so launch yourself across the gaps. The platforms are splattered all over, and the levels are built randomly every time, four of them, then a thank-you room. The tunnel is at the far end of the level, so keep heading away from where you started. Walk all the way through the dark tunnel and it leads straight into the next level, all the way up to level 5. You can pick a level to start at above.
Trigger throws a pebble to lure the creature away. Stone is silent, so the creature can’t hear you on the platforms. Only the water gives you away: slap it, splash through it, throw a pebble into it, or fall in. The water is shallow, so if you fall in you can wade to a platform and climb back out, but don’t hang around.
